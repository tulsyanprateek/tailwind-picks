How Momentum Radar works

A simple, rule-based way to own the strongest Nifty 100 stocks, only when the market is healthy.

In one minute
📅

Two check-in days a month

On the 1st and the 15th (or the next trading day). Nothing to do on other days.

🚦

First, check the whole market

If the Nifty 50 is weak, we don't buy anything and keep the money aside.

🏁

Pick the 5 fastest runners

Out of the Nifty 100, the 5 stocks that rose the most over the last 3, 6 and 12 months, and are still in a steady uptrend.

🛡️

Protect each one with a 15% stop loss

Set once on Kite. If the price falls that far, Kite sells it for you.

🔁

Keep the winners, swap the losers

On each check-in, keep stocks still in the Top 10. Sell the rest and buy new leaders.

Why buy stocks that already went up?

It sounds odd, but it is one of the most studied patterns in markets: stocks that rose the most over the last 3 to 12 months tend to keep doing better than the rest for a few more months. Big investors move money slowly, good news takes time to sink in, and people chase what is working. The NSE even runs an index built on this idea (Nifty200 Momentum 30).

It stops working sometimes, usually when the whole market turns. That is why we check the market first.

What are the 50-day and 200-day averages?

The 50-day average is the average closing price of the last 50 trading days (about 2.5 months). It shows the short-term direction.

The 200-day average is the same over 200 trading days (about 10 months). It shows the long-term direction.

Why these two? They are the most widely watched trend lines in the world, so many buyers and sellers react around them. A price above both, with the 50-day above the 200-day, means both the short and the long trend are up. A price below the 200-day often means the trend has broken.

Buy price, stop loss and target

Buy up to: last closing price + 2%. Place a limit order at this price before 9:15 am. If the stock opens higher and never comes back, skip it. Chasing a jump usually hurts.

Stop loss: 15% below your buy price, set as a GTT order on Kite. It caps the loss on one stock and works even when you are not watching.

Target: +25% is the typical gain of a winning trade in the 12-year test. It is a guide, not a sell order: winners are kept as long as they stay in the Top 10, and some go much further.

Sell everything when the market turns weak on a check-in day.

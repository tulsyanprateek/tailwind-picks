Read before trusting these numbers

Survivorship bias. The test uses today's Nifty 100 members for all 12 years. Stocks that fell out of the index (often after falling a lot) are missing, and today's winners are included from the start. Real results would likely be worse than shown.

Fundamentals filter not tested. Historical fundamentals are not available, so the backtest runs without the quality filter (module G). The live report can apply it.

Costs. Zerodha delivery charges and 0.1% slippage per side are included. Verify the rates at zerodha.com/charges.

Why the setting in use is not the best ratio: the test only contains stocks that are in the Nifty 100 today. Companies that collapsed and left the index (the situations a safety-net stop exists for) are missing, so the test cannot show what the stop protects against. The difference in the 2014 to 2020 results is small, so the safety net is kept as insurance. This was decided before looking at any 2021+ results for these versions.

The rules that were tested

  • Check-in on the first trading day on or after the 1st and 15th of each month. Picks use prices up to the previous close; orders go in at that day's open.
  • Only when the Nifty 50 is above its 200-day average and its 50-day average is above its 200-day average. Otherwise everything is sold and the money stays in cash.
  • Each stock must be in a healthy uptrend (above its 50, 150 and 200-day averages, the 200-day average rising, at least 30% above its 52-week low and within 25% of its 52-week high) and in the top 30% of the universe for 3, 6 and 12-month momentum.
  • Hold the 5 strongest. A holding is kept while it still qualifies and ranks in the top 10.
  • Each new holding gets 1/5 of the portfolio, bought with a limit order at last close + 2%.
  • Safety-net stop loss 15% below the buy price, as a GTT order on Kite. No target: holdings are sold at a check-in when they drop off the list.
  • If the stop and the target are both touched on the same day, the test assumes the stop was hit first.
